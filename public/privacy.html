Privacy Policy for Junior Tracker

Last updated: Aug 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Qrb Apps (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Junior Tracker mobile application (the “App”, package me.golovanov.juniortracker) and its backend service at tracker.golovanov.me.

Junior Tracker is a tool for parents and caregivers (adults) to log and review a baby’s daily activity (sleep, feeding, and similar events). The App is not directed to children and is not intended to be used by children.

Who we are

The data controller is Qrb Apps.
Contact: vladimirgolovanov@gmail.com.

What data we collect

Account data

  • Email address — used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Password — used to authenticate you. It is sent to our backend to create and access your account and is not stored on your device; we do not store it in plain text.

Child profile data (entered by you, the parent/caregiver)

  • Child’s name.
  • Child’s date of birth.
  • Time zone.

Activity data

  • Events you log (e.g. sleep, feeding, and other tracked activities), including their timestamps and any notes or values (such as volume) you add.

Technical & diagnostic data

  • Crash reports and diagnostic information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, and the technical context of an error), collected via our crash reporting provider (Sentry) to keep the App stable.

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and we do not use the App for advertising.

How we use your data

  • To provide the core features of the App (log events, show dashboards and charts).
  • To let multiple caregivers share access to the same child via an invite code.
  • To keep your account secure and synced across devices.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Legal basis (where applicable, e.g. EEA/UK)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (providing the App) and, for diagnostics, on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the App stable and secure.

Sharing and third parties

We do not share your personal data with third parties for their own purposes. We use the following service provider, acting on our behalf as a processor:

  • Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) — crash and error reporting. See Sentry’s privacy policy at https://sentry.io/privacy/.

Our backend is hosted on infrastructure at tracker.golovanov.me. Some providers (e.g. Sentry) may process data on servers located in the United States; by using the App you acknowledge this international transfer.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the App: Settings → Delete account, or
  • Via the web: https://tracker.golovanov.me/delete-account, or
  • By email: vladimirgolovanov@gmail.com.

Once deleted, your account data is removed from our systems; diagnostic data held by Sentry is retained according to Sentry’s own retention schedule.

Security

  • All traffic between the App and our backend is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Your authentication token is stored in encrypted storage on the device and is excluded from device backups.

Children’s privacy

Junior Tracker is intended for use by adults (parents and caregivers). It is not directed to children and does not knowingly allow children to create accounts. Information about a child is entered and controlled by the adult account holder.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: vladimirgolovanov@gmail.com
